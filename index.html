About Our Firm

Welcome to IDI Designs

IDI DESIGNS, a leading contracting and interior architecture firm, is driven by the dynamic combination of interior designing and its execution with perfection. Paired with an innovative and dedicated approach to design, documentation, and turnkey execution globally, IDI has become an internationally sought-after brand.

Established in New Delhi, India on 18th June 2010, IDI Designs Pvt. Ltd. has grown into an organization of more than 300 experienced professionals. With our registered corporate headquarters in New Delhi and branch offices in Mumbai, Bangalore, and Chennai—supported by dedicated site offices nationwide—we deliver consistent quality locally and at scale.
